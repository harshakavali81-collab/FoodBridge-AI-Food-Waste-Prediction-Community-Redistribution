# Human partner verification & food-safety onboarding

**Template / policy design only — no real partners were verified.** The current backend's `recipient.verified` and `food_safety_confirmed` fields belong to a fictional demonstration and **must not** be accepted as legal approval.

For an India-based pilot, use the official FSSAI **Food Safety and Standards (Recovery and Distribution of Surplus Food) Regulations, 2019** together with current amendments and the organization's food-safety adviser. Source: https://www.fssai.gov.in/food-law/regulations

## Verification workflow — human operator only

| Stage | Required evidence | Responsible party | Demo status |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organization identity | Legal entity, authorized representative, signed consent, contacts | Admin and partner | NOT VERIFIED |
| Regulatory status | FSSAI registration/licence details where applicable, validity and legal category | Compliance lead | NOT VERIFIED |
| Storage and transport | Equipment, sanitation, handling plans, documented temperature checks | Qualified food handler | NOT VERIFIED |
| Food traceability | Source, preparation/use-by times, ingredients, allergens, quantity, pickup and receipt records | Donor + receiver | NOT VERIFIED |
| Safety decision | Inspection, packaging, segregation, cold-chain, hazard review and rejection process | Authorized trained reviewer | NOT VERIFIED |
| Incident response | Recall workflow, complaints, contact tree and documented corrective action | Compliance lead | NOT VERIFIED |
| Authorization | Independent review, pilot contract, responsible contacts, stop/discard protocol | Organization lead | NOT VERIFIED |

## Per-donation checklist for human reviewers
- Food must be actually available, safely sourced and unserved where required, not a future model prediction.
- Preserve required original labeling on packaged food; for prepared food preserve required preparation/use-by and dietary labeling.
- Inspect contamination, storage temperature, packaging, allergens and permitted shelf-life and pickup timing.
- Confirm recipient verification and safe transport/storage with documented temperatures where applicable.
- Record pickup, receiver acknowledgment and corrective/recall events. Reject uncertain, mislabeled, recalled, expired or mishandled food.
- Retain only necessary records under documented privacy and retention policies.

The application's hard gates are **screening aids only**. They cannot test for bacterial contamination, perform lab inspection, decide medical suitability or satisfy legal recordkeeping without a separate verified workflow.

No real donor, NGO, redistribution success, compliance certification or approved pickup is claimed here.

## Primary food-law reference

Review the official [FSSAI Food Safety and Standards (Recovery and Distribution of Surplus Food) Regulations, 2019](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/Gazette_Notification_Surplus_Food_06_08_2019.pdf) and current amendments or applicable requirements with a qualified compliance professional. The rules address safe surplus food, handling, hygiene, packaging, transport, storage, expiry and responsibilities of food donors and distribution organizations. **Compliance has not been assessed or approved for this project.**
