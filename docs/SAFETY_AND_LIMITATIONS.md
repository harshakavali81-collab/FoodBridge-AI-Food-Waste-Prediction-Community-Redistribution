# Safety, privacy and validation boundaries

- All provided partners and history are **synthetic and fictional**.
- Donor acknowledgement is not a substitute for professional food handling inspection.
- A recipient's demonstration verification flag has not been independently certified.
- Humans must verify temperature control, allergens, recalls, packing, labeling, expiry, transport and suitability.
- Forecasts are estimates and **never** represent inventory or trigger donations automatically.
- Only explicit pickup confirmations count toward collected totals. Avoided-waste, emissions-saved and meals-fed claims require external validation.
- Straight-line distances are not driving times; demo radius is a screening heuristic.
- No production authentication, multi-tenant separation, audit-grade logs or production deployment is asserted.
- Do not upload sensitive beneficiary data. Apply local privacy and food-safety regulations before any real pilot.
